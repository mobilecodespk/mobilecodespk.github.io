Jazz Codes: Complete List of USSD Codes

Jazz is Pakistan's largest mobile network, and its subscribers use short USSD codes every day to check their balance, find their own number, subscribe to bundles and get help. This guide collects the well-known Jazz codes in one place, explains what each one does, and shows you how to use them safely. USSD codes are typed into your phone's dialler exactly as shown, starting with an asterisk and ending with the hash key, and then you press the call button. Most of them are free to dial, although some services, such as checking your balance, may carry a small charge that Jazz deducts from your account. Because Jazz updates its offers regularly, treat this page as a starting point and always confirm the current code and price in the Jazz World app before subscribing to anything.

Balance and account codes

The code most Jazz users know by heart is the balance check code. Dial *111# and your remaining balance appears on your screen within seconds. It is the fastest way to know how much credit you have left before making a call or buying a bundle, and it works on both prepaid and postpaid connections, although postpaid users will usually see their bill information in the app instead. If you want to know your own Jazz number because you have forgotten it or you are using a new SIM, dial *99# and your number is displayed. This is useful when someone asks for your number and you do not have it memorised, or when you are filling in a form and need the number quickly.

Internet and package codes

Jazz offers daily, weekly and monthly internet bundles, and the simplest way to explore them is through the Jazz World app, which lists every current bundle with its data allowance, validity and price. The app also lets you subscribe with one tap, which avoids typing long codes and removes the risk of subscribing to the wrong bundle by mistake. If you prefer codes, the general bundle menu is reached through the Jazz World app or by calling the helpline, because bundle codes change whenever Jazz launches a new offer. A useful habit is to check your remaining data after subscribing, so you know how much of your bundle is left and when it expires. The app shows remaining MBs, minutes and SMS in one place, and it also sends a notification when a bundle is about to expire.

Helpline and customer support

If a code does not work or you need help with your account, Jazz's helpline is 111 from a Jazz number. Calling the helpline connects you to an automated menu first, and from there you can reach a customer service representative. Keep your SIM number and CNIC details ready, because the representative may ask for them to verify your identity before discussing your account. Jazz also has service centres in most cities, where staff can help with SIM replacement, biometric verification and package problems that cannot be solved on the phone. For most everyday questions, though, the Jazz World app is quicker than calling, because it shows your balance, your active bundles and your usage history without waiting in a queue.

Tips for using Jazz codes safely

Only use codes from trusted sources such as this guide, the Jazz World app or Jazz's official helpline, and never dial a code sent to you in a suspicious SMS, because scam messages sometimes contain codes that forward your calls or subscribe you to paid services. If a code returns an error, check that you typed it exactly, with the asterisk at the start and the hash at the end, and try again after a minute. If it still fails, restart your phone and check that you have network signal. Remember that Jazz is a prepaid-first network for most users, so a bundle subscription will fail if your balance is lower than the bundle price, and topping up first will fix that. Finally, keep a note of the codes you use most often, so you do not have to search for them every time.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the Jazz balance check code?
A: Dial *111# from your Jazz number. Your remaining balance appears on the screen.

Q: How do I find my own Jazz number?
A: Dial *99# and your Jazz number will be displayed on your phone.

Q: What is the Jazz helpline number?
A: Dial 111 from a Jazz number to reach Jazz customer support.
