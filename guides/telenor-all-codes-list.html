Telenor Codes: Complete List of USSD Codes

Telenor Pakistan has served millions of subscribers for many years, and its USSD codes are part of daily life for its users. This guide gathers the well-known Telenor codes, explains what each one does, and gives you practical advice for using them. A USSD code is a short sequence you dial like a phone number: it starts with an asterisk, contains a few digits, and ends with the hash key. When you press call, the network runs the request and shows the result on your screen, all without using your mobile data. That is why USSD codes are so useful in areas with weak internet coverage, and why they still matter even though every operator now also has an app. Keep in mind that Telenor updates its bundles often, so confirm current offers in the My Telenor app.

Balance and own number

The Telenor balance check code is *444#. Dial it from your Telenor number and your remaining balance is displayed. It is a quick check to do before a long call or before subscribing to a bundle, and it helps you avoid the frustration of a bundle failing because your balance was too low. To find your own Telenor number, dial *710# and the number appears on your screen. This is useful with a new SIM, or when you are filling in a form and cannot remember the number. Both codes work on prepaid connections across Pakistan, wherever you have Telenor signal.

Internet and call bundles

Telenor sells a wide range of bundles, from small daily data packs to large monthly bundles and all-network call packages. The My Telenor app lists all of them with their data, minutes, validity and price, and subscribing through the app is the safest option because you can see exactly what you are buying before you confirm. Bundle codes do change, so a code that worked last month may have been replaced, and the app always reflects the current list. After subscribing, check your remaining balance of MBs and minutes in the app, and set a reminder for the expiry date so you can renew in time and avoid out-of-bundle charges.

Helpline and support

Telenor's helpline is 345 from a Telenor number. The helpline menu lets you choose the topic you need help with, and you can speak to a representative for account problems, package issues or network complaints. Keep your CNIC and SIM number nearby for verification. Telenor also has customer service centres in major cities for SIM replacement, biometric verification and other in-person services. The My Telenor app includes a help section and complaint tracking, which is useful if you want a record of your complaint and its reference number.

Practical tips

Always type codes exactly, and do not add spaces between the digits. If a code fails with an 'invalid MMI' message, restart your phone and try again, and check that your SIM is properly inserted and you have signal. Do not dial codes from suspicious messages, because they can activate services you did not ask for. If a bundle does not activate after you subscribe, wait a few minutes and check your active bundles in the app before trying again, because subscribing twice can charge you twice. If the problem continues, call 345 and ask the representative to check your subscription status.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.
