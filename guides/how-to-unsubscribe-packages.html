How to Unsubscribe from Mobile Packages

Subscribing to a mobile package is easy, but unsubscribing can feel harder, especially when a bundle renews automatically and charges you again before you notice. This guide shows you how to check which packages are active on your Jazz, Zong, Telenor or Ufone number, how to unsubscribe from them, and how to stop auto-renewal, so you only pay for what you actually want. The most important habit is to check your active bundles regularly in the official app, because many unwanted charges come from a bundle you forgot you had, not from a new subscription. If a bundle is charging you and you cannot unsubscribe, the helpline can do it for you.

First, check what is active on your number

Open the official app of your operator: Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone, and look at the bundles or subscriptions section, which lists every active package with its name, price, remaining allowance and expiry date. This is the most reliable check, because it shows everything, including bundles you may have forgotten. You will also usually receive an SMS when a bundle is subscribed or renewed, so searching your messages for the operator's name can reveal bundles you did not know about. If you see a bundle you do not recognise, do not ignore it: it may be renewing every week or month and charging you each time.

Unsubscribing in the app

In the official app, open the active bundle and look for an unsubscribe, deactivate or cancel option, which is usually shown on the bundle details screen. Tap it and confirm, and you will receive an SMS confirming that the bundle has been deactivated. Some bundles stop immediately when you unsubscribe, and some continue until their expiry date but do not renew, so read the confirmation message carefully to know which one applies to you. If the app does not show an unsubscribe option for a particular bundle, it may be a bundle that can only be cancelled by the helpline, so call them and ask. Never pay a third party who offers to unsubscribe you for a fee.

Stopping auto-renewal

Many bundles renew automatically when they expire, which is convenient if you want the same bundle every month, but expensive if you do not. In the app, check whether your bundle has auto-renewal turned on, and turn it off if you do not want it to renew. Some operators send a warning SMS before renewal, giving you a chance to cancel, so read those messages instead of deleting them. If a bundle has already renewed and you did not want it, call the helpline immediately: if you have not used the bundle, the agent may be able to help, although a refund is not guaranteed. To prevent future surprises, turn off auto-renewal on every bundle you buy, and renew manually when you need it.

If you cannot unsubscribe

If the app will not let you unsubscribe, call the helpline: 111 for Jazz, 310 for Zong, 345 for Telenor, 333 for Ufone, and ask the agent to deactivate the bundle. Have your CNIC ready for verification, and ask for a confirmation SMS. If the helpline cannot help, visit a service centre with your CNIC and ask in person. If you believe a bundle was subscribed without your permission, tell the helpline clearly, ask for the subscription details, including when and how it was subscribed, and ask for it to be blocked from renewing. Keep the complaint reference number, and follow up if the charge appears again. As a last resort, you can block premium subscriptions through the helpline, which stops third-party services from charging your balance.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I unsubscribe from a Jazz bundle?
A: Open Jazz World, find the active bundle and tap unsubscribe, or call 111 and ask the agent to deactivate it.

Q: How do I stop a bundle from renewing automatically?
A: Turn off auto-renewal in the official app, or ask the helpline to disable it for that bundle.

Q: Can I get a refund for a bundle I did not want?
A: Refunds are not guaranteed, but call the helpline immediately if you have not used the bundle; the agent may be able to help.
