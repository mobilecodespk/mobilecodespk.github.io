Roaming Activation Codes for All Networks

Roaming lets you use your Pakistani SIM when you travel abroad, so you can receive calls and SMS, and in some cases use mobile data, on a foreign network. It sounds simple, but roaming is one of the most expensive services a mobile user can buy by accident, because data used abroad is charged at a far higher rate than data at home, and a few minutes of browsing can cost thousands of rupees. This guide explains how roaming works for Jazz, Zong, Telenor and Ufone, how to activate it before you travel, and how to avoid a shocking bill when you come home. The golden rule is simple: sort out roaming before you leave Pakistan, not after you land.

What roaming is

When you travel abroad, your phone connects to a local network in the country you are visiting, through an agreement between your Pakistani operator and that foreign network. Your number stays the same, so people can still call and text you, and you can still call and text them, but the charges are set by the roaming agreement, not by your normal Pakistani rates. Incoming calls while roaming are usually charged, outgoing calls cost more than at home, and mobile data is the most expensive of all. SMS is usually the cheapest roaming service, and receiving SMS is often free, which is why many travellers keep roaming on only for SMS verification codes from their bank.

How to activate roaming before you travel

Roaming is not always active by default, and it is best to activate it before you leave. The reliable way is through your operator's official app or helpline: Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone, or helplines 111, 310, 345 and 333. The operator can confirm whether roaming is active on your SIM and which countries it covers. Some operators sell roaming bundles for popular destinations, which give you a set amount of data, minutes and SMS for a fixed price and a fixed number of days, and these are far cheaper than pay-as-you-go roaming. Buy the bundle before you travel, and check its validity and coverage, because a bundle for one country may not work in another.

Using your phone abroad without a huge bill

Turn off mobile data roaming in your phone settings as soon as you land, unless you have bought a roaming data bundle, and use Wi-Fi at your hotel instead. Turn off automatic app updates and cloud backups, which can use large amounts of data in the background without you noticing. Use WhatsApp calls over Wi-Fi for calls home, and use SMS only when you need to, because each SMS abroad costs more than at home. If you need local data, buying a local SIM at your destination is usually far cheaper than roaming data, although you will have a different number. Keep your Pakistani SIM in the phone for SMS verification codes, with data roaming off, and use the local SIM for data.

If roaming does not work abroad

If your phone shows no service abroad, first check that roaming is turned on in your phone settings, under Mobile Network or Cellular Data Options, where there is a Data Roaming switch. Then try manually selecting a network: go to network selection, turn off automatic selection, and choose one of the available networks. Restart the phone and try again. If it still does not work, check with your operator's helpline before you travel whether your SIM supports roaming in that country, because some destinations are not covered. If you are already abroad and cannot reach the helpline by phone, use Wi-Fi to contact your operator through its app or website.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I activate roaming on my Pakistani SIM?
A: Activate it before you travel through your operator's official app or helpline, and consider a roaming bundle for your destination.

Q: Is receiving SMS free while roaming?
A: Receiving SMS is often free, but check with your operator, because charges vary by country and agreement.

Q: Why is roaming data so expensive?
A: Data abroad is charged at roaming rates, far higher than home rates. Use Wi-Fi or a local SIM for data instead.
