Call Forwarding and Divert Codes

Call forwarding, also called call divert, sends your incoming calls to another number when you cannot answer them yourself. It is useful when your phone is switched off, when you are busy, when you are out of coverage, or when you want all your calls to go to a second number for a while. The codes for call forwarding are standard GSM codes, which means they work the same way on Jazz, Zong, Telenor and Ufone, and on most phones in the world. This guide explains each code, shows you how to turn forwarding on and off, and warns you about the charges, because forwarded calls are usually charged to you. Always test forwarding after setting it up, so you know it works before you rely on it.

Forward all calls

To forward all incoming calls to another number, dial **21*phone-number#, replacing 'phone-number' with the full number you want calls to go to, including the country code if it is an international number, and then press call. From that moment, every call to your number is sent straight to the other number, and your phone will not ring. To check whether forwarding is active, dial *#21#. To turn it off, dial ##21#. Use this when you are going on holiday and want a family member to take your calls, or when you have two phones and only want to carry one. Remember to turn it off when you are back, or you will miss calls without realising it.

Forward when busy, unanswered or unreachable

You do not have to forward every call: you can forward only in specific situations. To forward when your line is busy, dial **67*phone-number#, and turn it off with ##67#. To forward when you do not answer, dial **61*phone-number#, and turn it off with ##61#. To forward when your phone is switched off or out of coverage, dial **62*phone-number#, and turn it off with ##62#. These conditional options are the most useful, because your phone still rings normally most of the time, and calls are only forwarded when you genuinely cannot take them.

Checking and cancelling all forwarding

To see the status of each type of forwarding, dial *#21# for all calls, *#61# for unanswered, *#62# for unreachable, and *#67# for busy. To cancel all forwarding at once, dial ##002#, which turns off every type of forwarding and also cancels call barring and call waiting settings, so use it only when you want a clean reset. You can also manage forwarding in your phone's settings, under Call Settings or Supplementary Services, where the same options appear as menus instead of codes. If a code returns an error, try the phone settings menu instead, because some operators disable USSD forwarding codes on certain plans.

Charges and common mistakes

Forwarded calls are usually charged to the number that set up the forwarding, at the normal call rate to the forwarded number, so forwarding to another network or an international number can be expensive. Check with your operator before forwarding to a number you do not own, and never forward your calls to a number from a suspicious message, because scam messages sometimes contain forwarding codes that send your calls to a fraudster. A common mistake is typing the forwarded number without the correct format: use the full number as you would dial it normally. Another mistake is forgetting that forwarding is on, and then wondering why your phone never rings, so always check the status with the *# codes above after setting it up.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I forward all my calls to another number?
A: Dial **21*phone-number# and press call. Turn it off with ##21#.

Q: How do I stop all call forwarding?
A: Dial ##002# to cancel all forwarding types at once.

Q: Are forwarded calls charged?
A: Yes, usually at the normal call rate to the forwarded number, charged to you.
