SIM Activation Codes and How to Activate a New SIM

Buying a new SIM in Pakistan is simple, but the SIM does not work until it is activated, and activation now requires biometric verification by law. This guide walks you through the whole process, from buying the SIM to making your first call, for Jazz, Zong, Telenor and Ufone. It explains what documents you need, what happens at the shop, how long activation takes, and what to do if your SIM does not work after activation. There is no single magic activation code that works for every SIM, because activation is done by the shop or franchise through the operator's system after your fingerprint is verified, so this guide focuses on the real process rather than a code that does not exist.

What you need before you buy a SIM

You need your original CNIC, because a SIM in Pakistan can only be issued against a valid CNIC, and the shop will verify your fingerprint against the NADRA record. You cannot buy a SIM on someone else's CNIC, and you cannot activate a SIM without the CNIC holder being present for biometric verification. There is a limit on how many SIMs one CNIC can hold, so if you already have several SIMs, check how many are registered to your CNIC before buying another one. You can check this by sending your CNIC number by SMS to 668, which replies with the number of SIMs registered against it. Bring the CNIC itself, not a photocopy, because the biometric device needs the original card.

The activation process at the shop

At the shop or franchise, the seller enters your CNIC details and takes your fingerprint on a biometric device, which checks it against the NADRA database. If the fingerprint matches, the SIM is registered in your name and the seller activates it through the operator's system. The seller will usually insert the SIM into your phone and make a test call to confirm it works. The whole process takes a few minutes when the system is working, although it can take longer if the network is slow. You should receive a confirmation SMS on the new SIM once it is active. Keep the SIM packaging and the receipt, because they show your new number and the date of purchase.

How long activation takes

In most cases a new SIM becomes active within a few minutes to a few hours after biometric verification. Sometimes the operator asks you to wait up to 24 hours, especially if the SIM was bought late in the day. During this time the SIM may show signal but not allow calls, or it may show no service at all. Do not keep restarting the phone every minute; wait at least an hour, then restart once and try a call. If the SIM is still not working after 24 hours, return to the shop where you bought it with your CNIC and receipt, because the seller can check the activation status in the system. Do not buy a second SIM to fix the problem, because the first one may activate later and you will have paid twice.

If your new SIM does not work

First check that the SIM is inserted correctly and that your phone is not locked to another network. Restart the phone and check for signal. Try the SIM in another phone: if it works there, the problem is with your phone, not the SIM. If it does not work in any phone, call the helpline of the operator: 111 for Jazz, 310 for Zong, 345 for Telenor, 333 for Ufone, and ask whether the SIM is active in the system. The helpline can also check whether your biometric verification was completed. If the verification failed, you will need to visit the franchise again with your CNIC. Never pay anyone who promises to activate a SIM without biometric verification, because that is illegal and the SIM can be blocked later.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I activate a new SIM in Pakistan?
A: Buy it from an authorised shop or franchise with your original CNIC and complete biometric fingerprint verification. Activation usually completes within a few hours.

Q: How long does SIM activation take?
A: Usually minutes to a few hours, and up to 24 hours in some cases.

Q: Can I activate a SIM without biometric verification?
A: No. Biometric verification against your CNIC is legally required in Pakistan.
