International Calling Codes and Rates

Calling family and friends abroad is part of life for many Pakistanis, and international calls work differently from local calls. This guide explains how to dial an international number from Jazz, Zong, Telenor or Ufone, what the charges look like, and how to keep the cost down with bundles and internet calling. The most important thing to know is that international calls are never included in a local call package, and they are charged per minute at a rate that depends on the country you are calling. A long call to some countries can cost far more than you expect, so always check the rate before you dial, and consider whether a WhatsApp or other internet call would do the same job for free.

How to dial an international number

To call abroad from Pakistan, dial the international access code, then the country code, then the local number without its leading zero. The international access code is 00, or you can use the plus sign + instead, which works on most phones: for example, to call the United Kingdom, dial +44 followed by the UK number without its leading zero. Country codes are standard worldwide: 1 for the USA and Canada, 44 for the UK, 971 for the UAE, 966 for Saudi Arabia, and so on. If the call does not connect, check that you entered the country code correctly and that you did not include the local zero after it, because that is the most common dialling mistake.

International call rates

International rates vary widely by country and by operator, and they change from time to time, so this guide does not list prices, because a price printed here could be out of date tomorrow. The general pattern is that calls to popular destinations, such as the Gulf countries, the UK and the USA, are cheaper than calls to less common destinations, and some operators sell special international bundles for a fixed number of minutes to selected countries. Before a long international call, check the rate in your operator's official app or by calling the helpline, and make sure you have enough balance, because an international call will stop when your balance runs out. Never assume the rate is the same as a local call.

International bundles and cheaper options

If you call abroad regularly, ask your operator about international calling bundles, which give you a set number of minutes to selected countries for a fixed price. These are listed in the official app alongside local bundles, and they can be much cheaper than paying per minute. The cheapest option of all is usually an internet call through WhatsApp, Skype, FaceTime or a similar app, which uses your data or Wi-Fi instead of the mobile network, and costs nothing extra if you have a data bundle. Internet calls need both sides to be online and to have the same app, but for family calls they are the best choice. For calls to a landline or a person without internet, an international bundle is the next best option.

Common problems with international calls

If your international call fails, first check your balance, because international calls need credit even if you have a local call package. Check that international dialling is enabled on your SIM: some new SIMs have it disabled by default, and the helpline can enable it after verification. If the call connects but the quality is poor, try again later, because international routes can be congested at peak hours. If you are abroad and cannot call home, check whether roaming is active on your SIM, because that is a separate service covered in our roaming guide. Finally, beware of missed calls from unknown international numbers: never call them back, because they may be premium-rate numbers designed to charge you.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I call abroad from Pakistan?
A: Dial 00 or + followed by the country code and the number without its leading zero, e.g. +44 for the UK.

Q: Are international calls included in local call packages?
A: No. International calls are charged separately per minute, at a rate that depends on the country.

Q: What is the cheapest way to call abroad?
A: An internet call on WhatsApp or a similar app, or an international bundle from your operator for calls to landlines.
