How to Check Remaining MB, Minutes and SMS

Subscribing to a bundle is only half the job: you also need to know how much of it is left, and when it expires. This guide shows you how to check your remaining MBs, minutes and SMS on Jazz, Zong, Telenor and Ufone, so you are never surprised by a bundle running out in the middle of a call or a download. The easiest method is the official app, which shows everything in one place, but there are also code-based methods and SMS notifications to know about. Checking regularly helps you use your bundle fully before it expires, and it helps you decide whether to renew, buy an add-on, or switch to a different bundle next time.

Check in the official app

Open Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone, and your remaining data, minutes and SMS are shown on the home screen or in the bundles section. The app shows the exact remaining amount, the total you bought, and the expiry date and time, which is the information a USSD code often does not give you. The app also shows all your active bundles at once, which matters if you have more than one, for example a monthly data bundle and a weekly call package. Checking in the app is free, and it works over Wi-Fi as well as mobile data. Make it a habit to check once a day, or at least before a long call or a large download.

SMS notifications and balance codes

Most operators send you an SMS when you subscribe to a bundle, and many send another SMS when your bundle is nearly finished or has expired. Keep these messages, because they confirm what you bought and when it ends. The general balance code for your network also helps: Jazz *111#, Zong *222#, Telenor *444#, Ufone *124#. These codes show your balance, and on some networks they also show bundle information. If a code does not show your remaining MBs, use the app, which is the complete source. Do not rely on a code from an old article to check remaining data, because bundle-check codes change more often than balance codes.

What to do when your bundle runs out

If your data runs out, buy an add-on bundle in the app rather than continuing to browse, because out-of-bundle data is charged at a high rate and can drain your balance quickly. If your minutes run out, either buy another call package or top up your balance so calls are charged at the standard rate. If your SMS run out, buy a small SMS bundle or use WhatsApp instead. In every case, check the expiry date first: if the bundle has expired, your remaining MBs or minutes are lost and cannot be recovered, so renewing before expiry is better than renewing after. Some operators let you renew early in the app, and the new bundle starts when the old one ends.

Planning your usage so bundles last

Look at your usage pattern for a week: how much data you use per day, how many minutes you call, and how many SMS you send. Your phone's settings show data usage by app, which tells you whether video, social media or browsing uses the most. Use Wi-Fi for large downloads and video when it is available, and turn on data saver to stretch a bundle. Set a reminder two days before your bundle expires, so you can use the remaining data or renew in time. If you keep running out early, choose a bigger bundle next time; if you keep having data left over, choose a smaller one and save money.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I check my remaining MBs?
A: Open your operator's official app: it shows remaining data, minutes and SMS with the expiry date.

Q: Can I check remaining minutes by code?
A: Balance codes (*111# Jazz, *222# Zong, *444# Telenor, *124# Ufone) show balance; for full bundle details use the app.

Q: What happens to unused MBs when a bundle expires?
A: Unused data in an expired bundle is usually lost, so use it or renew before the expiry date.
