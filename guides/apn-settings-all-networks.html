APN Settings for Jazz, Zong, Telenor and Ufone

APN settings are the small set of details your phone uses to connect to your operator's mobile internet. When they are correct, your mobile data works. When they are wrong or missing, you can have full signal and still be unable to open a website, even though calls and SMS work fine. This guide explains what APN settings are, lists the well-known APN names for Jazz, Zong, Telenor and Ufone, and shows you how to enter them on Android and iPhone. Most phones set the APN automatically when you insert a SIM, so you will only need this guide if your data has stopped working after a new SIM, a phone reset, or a software update. If in doubt, ask your operator's helpline to send the settings to your phone automatically.

What is an APN?

APN stands for Access Point Name, and it is the gateway address your phone uses to reach the internet through your mobile network. Think of it as the address your phone dials to get online: without the right address, the network does not know where to send your internet traffic. The APN name is the most important field, and for most Pakistani networks the other fields, such as username and password, are left blank, because the network authenticates your SIM automatically. Your phone usually receives the correct APN by SMS from the operator when you first insert the SIM, and it saves it without you doing anything. Problems start when that automatic setup fails, which happens most often with a new phone, a phone bought abroad, or after a factory reset.

APN names for each network

The widely published APN names are as follows, and they should be entered exactly, in lower case, with no spaces. For Jazz, the common APN is jazzconnect.mobilinkworld.com, and some phones also work with the simpler APN internet. For Zong, the common APN is zonginternet. For Telenor, the common APN is internet, and some settings also list telenor. For Ufone, the common APN is ufone.internet, and some phones also work with internet. If one APN does not work, try the alternative listed for your network, and if neither works, call the helpline and ask for the settings to be sent to your phone.

Setting up APN on Android

On Android, open Settings, then go to Connections or Network and Internet, then Mobile Network, then Access Point Names. Tap the plus or Add button to create a new APN. Enter a name, such as your operator's name, and enter the APN name from the list above. Leave the other fields blank unless your operator tells you otherwise. Save the APN, then tap it to select it as the active one, and turn mobile data off and on again. Your data should start working within a minute. If it does not, restart the phone, and check that mobile data is turned on and that you have an active data bundle or balance.

Setting up APN on iPhone

On iPhone, APN settings are usually set automatically by the carrier settings, and the menu to change them is often hidden. Go to Settings, then Mobile Data or Cellular, then Mobile Data Network, and if the APN field is visible, enter the APN name for your network. If the menu is not visible, your iPhone is using the operator's carrier profile, and you cannot change the APN manually. In that case, update your carrier settings by going to Settings, General, About, and accepting any carrier update offered, or reset network settings under Settings, General, Transfer or Reset, Reset Network Settings, which will re-download the correct settings. If data still does not work, call the helpline.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the Jazz APN?
A: The widely published Jazz APN is jazzconnect.mobilinkworld.com; some phones also work with 'internet'.

Q: What is the Zong APN?
A: The widely published Zong APN is zonginternet.

Q: My signal is full but the internet does not work. Is it the APN?
A: It can be. Check the APN settings for your network, or ask the helpline to send the settings to your phone.
