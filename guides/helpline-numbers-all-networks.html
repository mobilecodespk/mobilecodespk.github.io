Helpline Numbers for Jazz, Zong, Telenor and Ufone

When a code does not work, a bundle does not activate, or a charge appears that you do not recognise, the fastest help is usually your operator's helpline. This guide lists the helpline number for each of Pakistan's four main mobile networks, explains what each helpline can and cannot do, and gives you practical tips for getting your problem solved in one call instead of three. Keep this page saved, because helpline numbers are the kind of thing you need suddenly, often when you are already frustrated, and searching for the number at that moment wastes time. Remember that helpline calls may be charged, and that you will need to verify your identity before the agent discusses your account.

The four helpline numbers

Jazz helpline is 111 from a Jazz number. Zong helpline is 310 from a Zong number. Telenor helpline is 345 from a Telenor number. Ufone helpline is 333 from a Ufone number. Dial the number from your own SIM on that network, and you will first hear an automated menu. Listen to the options and choose the one closest to your problem, because choosing the wrong department means being transferred, which takes longer. If you are calling from another network or a landline, the helpline may have a different number, which is listed on the operator's official website or in its app.

What the helpline can help with

Helpline agents can check your balance and active bundles, explain a charge on your account, activate or deactivate a service, and register a complaint about network coverage or a failed bundle. They can also guide you through SIM services, such as blocking a lost SIM, and they can tell you the location of the nearest service centre. What they cannot do is change the price of a bundle, refund a bundle you have already used, or fix a problem with your phone's hardware. For those, you need the service centre or the phone's manufacturer. Agents also cannot discuss an account without verifying the owner, so have your CNIC number ready.

Getting help faster

Before you call, write down your problem in one sentence, your mobile number, and the time the problem happened. If it is about a bundle, note the bundle name, the price, and the confirmation SMS you received. Call during off-peak hours, such as mid-morning on a weekday, when waiting times are shorter. Speak calmly and clearly, and ask for a complaint reference number if your issue is registered as a complaint, because that number lets you follow up later. If the agent cannot solve the problem, ask politely to speak to a supervisor or ask what the next step is, and note the name of the person you spoke to and the time of the call.

Alternatives to calling the helpline

Every operator's official app has a help or support section, and for many problems it is faster than calling. The app can show your bundles, your balance and your usage, and it lets you raise a complaint and track it with a reference number. Operators also have service centres and franchises in most cities, where staff can help with biometric verification, SIM replacement and ownership transfer, which cannot be done on the phone. Social media pages and official websites list service centre locations and opening hours. Use the helpline for urgent account problems, the app for everyday checks, and the service centre for anything that needs your physical presence.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the Jazz helpline number?
A: Dial 111 from a Jazz number.

Q: What is the Zong helpline number?
A: Dial 310 from a Zong number.

Q: What are the Telenor and Ufone helpline numbers?
A: Telenor is 345 and Ufone is 333, dialled from a number on that network.
