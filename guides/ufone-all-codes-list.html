Ufone Codes: Complete List of USSD Codes

Ufone is one of Pakistan's oldest mobile networks, and its subscribers rely on USSD codes for the small tasks that come up every day: checking a balance, finding their own number, buying a bundle, or reaching customer support. This guide collects the well-known Ufone codes in one place and explains how to use each one. USSD codes are dialled from the phone app like a normal call. You enter the code, press the call button, and the network answers on your screen, usually within seconds, without needing mobile data. That makes them reliable even in areas where the internet is slow. Ufone refreshes its bundles and offers from time to time, so use this guide as a reference and confirm the current code and price in the My Ufone app before subscribing.

Balance and own number

The Ufone balance check code is *124#. Dial it from your Ufone number and your remaining balance appears on the screen. It is worth checking before you subscribe to any bundle, because a bundle will fail if your balance is lower than its price. To find your own Ufone number, dial *780# and the number is displayed. This helps when you have a new SIM and have not memorised the number yet, or when a form asks for your number and you want to be sure it is correct. Both codes work wherever you have Ufone coverage in Pakistan.

Internet and call bundles

Ufone offers daily, weekly and monthly internet bundles, call packages and SMS bundles, and the full current list is in the My Ufone app, with each bundle's allowance, validity and price. Subscribing through the app is recommended because you can read the details before confirming, and the app shows your active bundles afterwards so you can track what is left. Bundle codes change when Ufone launches new offers, so a code from an old source may no longer work, and the app is the most reliable place to check. If you use data heavily, a monthly bundle usually works out cheaper per GB than repeated daily bundles, but the right choice depends on how much data you actually use.

Helpline and support

Ufone's helpline is 333 from a Ufone number. The automated menu guides you to the right department, and you can speak to an agent for billing questions, package problems or network issues. Have your CNIC and SIM number ready, because the agent may ask for them to verify your identity. Ufone service centres handle SIM replacement, biometric verification and ownership changes in person. The My Ufone app also has a support section where you can raise a complaint and follow its progress without making a call.

Using Ufone codes safely

Dial codes exactly as written, with the asterisk first and the hash last, and do not add spaces. If a code returns an error, check your signal, restart your phone, and try once more, because the error is often temporary. Never dial a code that arrives in a suspicious SMS or WhatsApp message, because such codes can forward your calls or subscribe you to a paid service. If a bundle subscription fails, check your balance and your active bundles in the app before retrying, so you do not pay twice for the same bundle. If the problem continues, call 333 and ask for help.

Important: USSD codes and package offers change from time to time. Confirm the current code and price in your operator's official app (Jazz World, My Zong, My Telenor or My Ufone) before you subscribe.
