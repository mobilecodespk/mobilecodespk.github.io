About

Mobile Codes Pakistan is an independent, unofficial guide to mobile network codes in Pakistan. We cover Jazz, Zong, Telenor and Ufone, and our aim is simple: to help people find the right USSD code, package or helpline number quickly, without guessing and without falling for fake codes shared on social media.

Everything on this site is written from scratch by our team. We only list well-known, widely published codes, and we label all package prices as something to confirm in the official app, because offers change often. We do not sell SIMs, we do not activate packages for anyone, and we do not represent any telecom company.

If you spot a code that no longer works, please tell us through the contact page so we can check it and update the guide.

Mobile Codes Pakistan — Unofficial & independent. Not affiliated with Jazz, Zong, Telenor, Ufone or any telecom company. Codes, packages and prices change often: always confirm the current code and price in the official app of your operator before subscribing or dialling.
